# LetX ERP Homepage Image Replacement Guide

This document lists all the placeholder template images currently used on your `index.html` (Homepage) that you will need to replace with your own custom LetX ERP product screenshots, client logos, and team/office photos. 

I have purposefully excluded small UI elements like checkmarks, UI background shapes, and SVG icons since those are part of the core design. 

## How to replace them
Simply copy your new images into the specified folders, ensure they have the exact same filename (e.g. `hero-1.jpg`), and overwrite the existing ones. Maintaining the same approximate aspect ratio for your new images will ensure the layout doesn't stretch or break.
