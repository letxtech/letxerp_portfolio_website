# NFM Kitchen

NFM Kitchen needed a lightweight ERP approach for restaurant operations, POS activity, daily sales tracking, and clearer control over items used in the business.

LetX ERP supported the client with a Starter ERP rollout focused on practical restaurant workflows. The goal was to keep the system simple, fast for users, and useful for daily decision-making.

## What We Delivered

- Restaurant POS workflow setup
- Sales and item tracking structure
- Basic stock visibility for daily operations
- User-friendly screens for fast adoption
- Training and rollout guidance

## Result

NFM Kitchen received a focused Starter ERP foundation that helps the team manage restaurant sales and daily activity with better visibility and less manual tracking.
