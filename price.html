Pricing & Package FAQs

Straightforward answers about Desktop POS licensing, Odoo implementation scope, customization, LKR quotations in Sri Lanka, and enterprise deployments.

Q: Are there any recurring monthly fees for the Desktop package?

No. The Desktop package is a fixed $119 one-time payment for one computer license with remote installation support and zero monthly subscription fees.

Q: Why are Essential and Core listed with starting from prices?

Businesses differ in branch count, user seats, hosting needs, and custom workflows. Starting prices give an accessible baseline while final pricing scales transparently with your scope. You can also explore real ERP client projects to see how packages fit.

Q: What is covered under the Core package's $359 initial setup fee?

The $359 initial setup covers ERP consulting and business process mapping, branded invoice and quotation templates, user role permissions, data onboarding support, and team training.

Q: Can Core and Advanced include Odoo customization and ERP integrations?

Yes. Both Core and Advanced support Odoo ERP implementation, custom Odoo module development, business process automation, and third-party API bridges for e-commerce, payment, and messaging platforms.

Q: Do you offer ERP implementations and LKR quotes in Sri Lanka as well as Dubai and international clients?

Yes. LetX Technologies operates from Turnor Road, Colombo 08, Sri Lanka with a branch in Dubai, UAE. Sri Lankan businesses can request LKR-denominated proposals and direct WhatsApp demos, while international clients can deploy remotely on USD plans.

Q: How do I obtain a quotation for the Advanced Enterprise suite?

Click "Talk to Enterprise Team" or message us on WhatsApp (or visit our contact page) to arrange an ERP consulting discovery session and receive a tailored, itemized quotation.
