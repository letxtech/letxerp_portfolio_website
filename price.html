Offline POS System

Desktop — 100% Offline Point of Sale Software

A dedicated, fully offline business counter software installed directly on your business computer, guaranteeing continuous billing with zero internet dependence.

One-Time Fixed Price
$119/one-time
1 Device License • Lifetime Use • No Monthly Fees
Get Desktop POS

Business Operational Fit

Ideal For: Single-counter retail shops, grocery stores, mini-marts, pharmacies, boutique outlets, and cafes wanting a high-speed, dependable billing software without monthly overheads or internet reliance.

Data & Hosting Architecture

100% Local PC Processing: All transaction records, customer data, and item registries stay strictly on your local computer. Operates completely without internet connectivity or external cloud servers.

What is Included in the $119 Price

  • Full POS Software License: Permanent lifetime license for 1 computer.
  • Fast Counter Checkout: Instant barcode scanning, multiple payment methods, and receipt printing.
  • Local Inventory & Stock: Real-time item lookup, low stock alerts, and daily sales summaries.
  • Installation Support Included: Remote installation assistance, hardware configuration guidance, and initial staff walkthrough.

Optional Extensions & Additions

Additional computer devices require an additional device license ($119/device). POS hardware (receipt printers, barcode scanners, cash drawers) is supported standard and supplied or connected independently.

Pricing & Package FAQs

Straightforward answers to common questions about our ERP pricing, licensing, and implementation services.

Q: Are there any recurring monthly fees for the Desktop package?

No. The Desktop package has one fixed price of $119 as a one-time payment. There are no monthly or yearly subscription fees. It includes remote installation support and initial user guidance for one computer device.

Q: Why are Essential and Core listed with "starting from" prices?

Every business operates with different branch counts, team sizes, and feature needs. The displayed rates provide an accessible starting baseline for standard operations, while final pricing scales transparently if you require custom workflows, additional branches, extra user seats, or dedicated hosting.

Q: What is covered under the Core package's $359 initial setup fee?

The $359 initial setup covers professional business process mapping, document branding (invoices, quotes, purchase orders), user role permission configuration, master data assistance, and hands-on onboarding sessions with your team.

Q: How do I obtain a quotation for the Advanced Enterprise package?

Simply click "Contact Our Team" or reach out via WhatsApp/email. Our ERP consultants will schedule a brief operational discovery call to understand your processes, multi-company structure, and integrations, and deliver a tailored, itemized quotation.
