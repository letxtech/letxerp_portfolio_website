Starter ERP

$149/mo

Starter ERP is our basic custom ERP for small and growing teams that need a clean way to manage daily operations without a heavy implementation. It focuses on essential workflows such as sales, invoicing, inventory, customer records, and basic reporting.

  • Best for teams moving away from spreadsheets and manual tracking.
  • Useful for businesses that need a fast, lightweight ERP foundation.
  • Can be improved later as your processes become more structured.

Odoo Starter

$279/mo

Odoo Starter is for companies that want to begin with Odoo using core modules and guided setup. It is a good fit when you need standard ERP workflows, basic configuration, and user onboarding without deep custom development at the first stage.

  • Best for companies starting their first Odoo rollout.
  • Covers core setup, module configuration, and basic workflows.
  • Helps teams understand Odoo before expanding into advanced features.

Odoo Pro

$479/mo

Odoo Pro is for businesses that need more than standard setup. This package supports multi-module rollout, custom addon planning, approval workflows, advanced reports, and stronger implementation support for department-level operations.

  • Best for businesses with custom processes and approval flows.
  • Supports reports, custom features, and practical workflow improvements.
  • Designed for teams that need better control across departments.

Odoo Ultra

$849/mo

Odoo Ultra is the complete ERP rollout path for companies that need advanced departments, roles, integrations, deeper customization, reporting, training, and dedicated launch assistance. It is built for long-term ERP adoption and scale.

  • Best for businesses ready for a complete ERP transformation.
  • Supports advanced configuration, integrations, and rollout planning.
  • Includes focused guidance for launch, adoption, and improvements.
