About LetX ERP

LetX ERP is the ERP solution division of LetX Technologies. Since 2023, we have helped businesses replace scattered manual work with connected digital operations.

We work with trusted clients across manufacturing, restaurants, import and export, pharmacy, supermarket, and other operational industries. These businesses need clearer control over sales, purchasing, inventory, finance, approvals, users, and reporting.

For lean requirements we provide Starter ERP, a lightweight custom ERP foundation. For broader operations we support Odoo ERP packages with consulting, configuration, customization, and rollout guidance.

Process First

We map how your business works before shaping modules, approvals, dashboards, and user roles.

Right ERP Path

We help you choose between Starter ERP and Odoo ERP based on your real operational needs.

Useful Visibility

We focus on reports and dashboards that support faster decisions, not only software screens.

ERP Members

The core roles that guide LetX ERP projects from discovery to rollout.

ERP Business Consultant

Understands requirements, workflows, pain points, and business priorities.

Odoo Consultant

Shapes Odoo flows, modules, settings, and user adoption plans.

Odoo Developer

Builds custom features, reports, integrations, and technical improvements.

Project Manager

Keeps scope, communication, delivery stages, and rollout timelines aligned.

LetX ERP helps businesses move daily operations into clear, connected systems with Starter ERP and Odoo ERP packages.

Built by LetX Technologies for teams that need practical ERP guidance and dependable rollout support.
