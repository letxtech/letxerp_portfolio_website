5+ Years of ERP Consulting & Engineering by LetX Technologies

LetX ERP is the dedicated ERP division of LetX Technologies. Since 2021, our team in Colombo 08, Sri Lanka—with a branch presence in Dubai, UAE—has helped growing businesses replace disconnected manual work with structured digital operations.

We work alongside Sri Lankan businesses across restaurants, hardware retail, motor parts shops, trading, distribution, and multi-branch operations—including real client rollouts for NFM Kitchen, MBS, and Maxpo Lanka. Every team needs dependable control over sales, POS, purchasing, inventory, accounting, approvals, and reporting.

Whether you start with our offline-ready Desktop POS or cloud Essential ERP, or scale into customized Core and Advanced deployments, we deliver customized ERP development, Odoo ERP implementation, Odoo customization, business automation, and ongoing support. Explore our ERP & Odoo planning guides or consult our Colombo team.
